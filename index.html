01 · The event

Build ahead. Assemble overnight. Demo live.

εὖLife 2027 is hosted at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), anchored by a theme inspired by LG's global vision of elevating everyday human experiences through connected, intelligent and sustainable technologies.

30 shortlisted teams from startups and academic research cohorts design their software modules and hardware components beforehand. The 24-hour on-campus sprint is used exclusively to assemble, integrate, test and demonstrate.

  1. Pitch-deck pre-screeningApply with your concept. 30 teams are shortlisted, plus 5 on the waitlist.
  2. Pre-buildDevelop your modules and hardware ahead of the event.
  3. 24-hour assembly sprintIntegrate, test and harden at CUSAT with mentor rounds.
  4. Live booth demoPresent to the jury, booth by booth, on Day 2.

        09 · Good to know

        Questions

        Can we write code during the 24 hours?

        The sprint is for assembly, integration, testing and demonstration. Software modules and hardware components are expected to be pre-built before you arrive. Code and hardware freeze at 07:30 AM on Day 2.

        Who can apply?

        Startups (registered entities or pre-incubation, with an active MVP) and enrolled UG/PG/PhD students with a faculty mentor. Teams have 2 to 5 members.

        Is food provided?

        Yes: four meals plus tea and snacks across the 24 hours, and every participant gets an event kit and badge.

        Do both cohorts compete against each other?

        No. The two cohorts are judged independently, each with its own ₹50,000 prize pool.

        Where is it?

        On campus at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), Kochi, Kerala, hosted by the LGSI-CUSAT Smart Connectivity Lab.
